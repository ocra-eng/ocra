Obstacle sport involves athletes, children and young people, coaches, Technical
Officials, clubs, race organisers and volunteers. Policies exist so everyone knows
the standard expected of them, and so there is a known process when something goes
wrong rather than an improvised one.

These sit alongside the [Constitution and Bylaws](/governance/constitution-bylaws)
and the competition [rules](/compete/rules).

## The policies

Each is published in full on its own page. **What is on the page is what is in
force** — there is no separate document to request.

[Safeguarding](/governance/safeguarding)
: Keeping children and young people safe, what is required of adults working with
them, and how to raise a concern.

[Anti-Doping](/governance/anti-doping)
: Clean sport, testing, medication and supplements, and the Sport Ireland rules
that apply.

[Codes of Conduct](/governance/codes-of-conduct)
: The standard of behaviour expected of athletes, coaches, officials, volunteers,
club officers, parents and spectators.

[Complaints & Disciplinary](/governance/complaints)
: How to raise a complaint, how it is handled, and what outcomes are possible.

[Equality & Inclusion](/governance/equality-inclusion)
: Who the sport is for, and what we do about the barriers.

[Data Protection](/governance/data-protection)
: What personal information OCRA holds, why, how long, and your rights over it.
This page is also OCRA's privacy notice.

## Two areas not yet standalone

Conflicts of interest is currently covered inside
[governance](/governance/structure). Competition integrity is covered inside
[rules & regulations](/compete/rules). Both work where they are; both would be
clearer as policies in their own right, and will be.

## Review

Policies are reviewed as OCRA develops and as legal, sporting or regulatory
requirements change. Where one is revised, the page carries the change and the
date it took effect.
