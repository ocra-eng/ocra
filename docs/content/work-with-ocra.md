Race organisers create the events that introduce thousands of people to obstacle
racing, give experienced athletes somewhere to compete, and build the community
around the sport.

OCRA's aim is not to replace independent organisers or take control of existing
events. It is to strengthen the Irish calendar as a whole, and to connect
recreational racing, competitive sport, National Championships and international
qualification into something an athlete can navigate.

## You choose the depth

Not every organiser wants the same relationship, and none of these obliges you to
the next one:

- List your event on the OCRA calendar
- Promote it to the Irish OCR community
- Work with local clubs and athletes
- Apply for [recognition or sanctioning](/race-organisers/sanctioning)
- Become an approved Qualification Event
- Work with OCRA [Technical Officials](/education/technical-officials)
- Develop competitive categories within a recreational event
- Explore hosting an Irish National Championship
- Connect your results with OCRA results systems
- Build a longer-term partnership

## Your event stays yours

Events do not need to be organised by OCRA to be part of Irish obstacle racing.

Organisers remain responsible for the management, commercial operation and
delivery of their own events unless something different has been specifically
agreed. Working with OCRA is meant to add opportunities, not remove your
independence.

## Competitive pathways

OCRA develops the Irish pathways into recognised national and international
competition, and independent organisers can become part of them. Suitable events
may be considered for sanctioning, Qualification Event status, National
Championship hosting and future ranking inclusion.

Each of those must be agreed in advance. Listing your event on the calendar
creates none of them —
[Event Recognition & Sanctioning](/race-organisers/sanctioning) sets out what each
requires.

## A calendar athletes can read

Working together, the calendar can tell an athlete what events exist, where they
can race, which are recreational, which offer competitive categories, which are
sanctioned, which provide international qualification, and where the National
Championships are.

That is worth more to the sport than any single event, including ours.

## Technical Officials

Competitive events can work with OCRA Technical Officials, who bring consistency
to competition rules, obstacle completion, penalties, competition procedures,
results verification and protest processes. The level of involvement depends on
the type and status of the event.

## Let's talk

You do not need to wait until your event is fully formed. Whether you run an
established race, are developing something new, or just want to know how your
event could fit the wider calendar, [get in touch](/about/contact).
