The Obstacle Course Racing Association of Ireland — OCRA ÉIREANN — is the
national organisation for the development and promotion of obstacle sport in
Ireland. Everywhere else on this site we just say OCRA.

Our role runs the whole width of the sport, from someone trying their first
obstacle race to an athlete representing Ireland at a World Championship. We are
a non-profit, volunteer-led organisation, incorporated as a company limited by
guarantee.

## What we do

- **Athletes.** Pathways from first race to international competition, the Irish
  [National Championships](/compete/championships), and
  [qualification and selection](/compete/qualification) for the athletes who
  represent Ireland.
- **Clubs and communities.** Supporting the clubs that already exist and helping
  new ones [start](/clubs/start-a-club).
- **Coaches and officials.** A [coaching pathway](/coaching) and a
  [Technical Officials](/education/technical-officials) programme, so standards
  mean the same thing at every competition.
- **Events.** Working with race organisers to strengthen the Irish calendar
  rather than competing with it.
- **The sport itself.** Safe, fair and inclusive participation, and the
  [governance](/governance/structure) to hold ourselves to it.

## Our community

Obstacle racing draws people from a wide range of sporting backgrounds and
ability levels. Some compete at elite international level. Others race
recreationally, train with a local club, volunteer at events, coach, officiate,
or simply enjoy taking on obstacles with friends.

OCRA exists to support all of that — which means clear ways to find somewhere to
train, take part in events, develop skills, and where people choose it, move into
competing, coaching, officiating or organising.

## Building the sport together

The sport develops through collaboration, not by building each part of it in
isolation. We would rather work with athletes, clubs, training facilities, race
organisers, coaches, officials and volunteers than around them.

Whether you are taking on your first obstacle race, building a local community,
organising an event or aiming to represent Ireland, there is a route here.
[New to the sport?](/about/what-is-ocr) starts at the beginning.
