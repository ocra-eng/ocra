OCRA works with organisations that want to support the development of obstacle
sport in Ireland — creating better opportunities for athletes, clubs, coaches,
events and communities.

We are a volunteer-led non-profit, so partnership money goes into the sport rather
than into overhead.

## Who we work with

Businesses, sports organisations, race organisers, training facilities,
educational organisations, local authorities, community organisations, sports
technology providers, equipment suppliers, media, health and fitness
organisations, and tourism and destination partners.

## What a partnership can support

**Team Ireland** — team clothing, athlete preparation, travel, equipment, team
operations and championship support. International competition is where Irish
athletes are most visible and least funded.

**Events and competition** — sponsorship of OCRA events, qualification races and
National Championships, plus equipment, venues, technical services, timing,
athlete services, media and awards.

**Grassroots** — clubs, coaching, youth programmes, training opportunities,
community events, volunteer development, and inclusion and adaptive sport. Less
visible than a championship, and where the long-term future of the sport is
decided.

## Beyond sponsorship

If your organisation has expertise, technology, facilities, services or ideas that
could help develop the sport, we are more interested in that conversation than in
a logo placement.

Some of the most useful things a partner can provide are not money.

## How we choose

OCRA works with organisations whose activities and values are compatible with
sport, athlete welfare and the long-term interests of the obstacle racing
community.

A partnership should provide clear value to both sides without compromising
sporting independence or integrity — and we will say no to arrangements that
would.

## Talk to us

[Get in touch](/about/contact) with something about your organisation and the kind
of partnership you have in mind. If you are not sure what is possible, that is a
reasonable place to start from.
