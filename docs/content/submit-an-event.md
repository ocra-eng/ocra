If you organise an OCR event, obstacle challenge or suitable related competition
in Ireland, you can submit it for the OCRA Events Calendar.

Submissions are welcome from established organisers, new organisers, clubs,
training facilities and other organisations delivering suitable obstacle racing.

## What we need from you

The event name and organiser, the date and location, your website and
registration link, a contact name and email, the race distances or formats you are
running, whether there are adult and youth options, the minimum age, whether
categories are competitive or recreational, a short description, and a logo or
image if you have one.

If a detail is not settled yet, send what you have — an approximate date on the
calendar is more use to athletes than nothing.

## OCRA status

Tell us on the form if you want to discuss sanctioning, Qualification Event
status, National Championship opportunities, Technical Official support, or
another kind of collaboration.

:::before-you-rely-on-this
Submitting to the calendar does **not** apply for any OCRA status and does not
grant one. A listing means the event exists and is relevant to Irish OCR — nothing
more. [Event Recognition & Sanctioning](/race-organisers/sanctioning) explains
what each status actually involves.
:::

## What happens to your submission

OCRA reviews submissions before publication to check they are relevant to the
Irish obstacle racing community, and comes back to you if anything is missing.

Being listed on ocra.ie does not mean OCRA operates, endorses or sanctions the
event unless a specific status is displayed alongside it.

## If something changes

Tell us. Date, venue, registration, cancellation, race formats, contact details —
all of it affects athletes who are planning around your event, sometimes months
ahead and often with travel booked.

Keeping a listing accurate is a small job for you and a significant one for them.
