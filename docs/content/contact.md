We welcome enquiries from athletes, clubs, coaches, Technical Officials, race
organisers, volunteers, partners and anyone thinking about getting involved.

Use the form and pick the closest enquiry type — that is what routes your message
to the right person rather than a general inbox. For anything else,
`info@ocra.ie` reaches us.

## Some enquiries have their own route

Three categories go somewhere specific, and picking them gets you a faster and
better-informed answer:

Complaint or disciplinary matter
: [Complaints & Disciplinary](/governance/complaints) sets out what to include
before you write.

Data protection or privacy
: [Data Protection](/governance/data-protection) covers what we hold and your
rights over it.

Qualification or Team Ireland
: Check [Qualification](/compete/qualification) first — most questions are
answered there, and if yours is not we will know that quickly.
