Ireland has a growing calendar of obstacle racing, from first-timer events
through to qualification races for international championships.

Alongside races organised by OCRA, the calendar includes independent events such
as Hard as Oak, Rampage, Hell & Back, Spartan and Turf Warrior. OCRA works
alongside their organisers rather than in competition with them — a bigger
calendar is the point.

## Find your next race

Irish obstacle racing runs from large recreational mud runs to technical
competitive races. Distances, obstacle difficulty, categories and rules all vary
between organisers, so read the individual event information before you register.

The OCRA Events Calendar pulls these together and shows which events are
recreational, which are competitive, and which count towards qualification.

## Irish National Championships

OCRA may run National Championships directly or with an established race
organiser. Either way they are identified in the calendar, with the disciplines
and categories on offer.

See [Championships](/compete/championships) for what is currently open.

## Organise an event?

OCRA would rather grow the whole sport than only run its own races. See
[working with OCRA](/race-organisers/work-with-ocra) for the ways an event can
connect to the national pathway, or
[submit an event](/race-organisers/submit-an-event) to get it on the calendar.
