:::escalate
If a child appears to be in immediate danger, their safety comes first. Contact
An Garda Síochána directly.

Child protection concerns can also be reported to **Tusla**, the Child and Family
Agency, and to **An Garda Síochána**, whether or not you also tell OCRA. You do
not need our permission and you do not need to be certain — reporting a concern
is not the same as making an accusation.

A safeguarding concern is never handled as an ordinary sporting complaint.
:::
