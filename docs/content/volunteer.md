Obstacle sport in Ireland runs on people giving their time. Volunteers make events
happen, support athletes, build communities and deliver the programmes that let
the sport grow.

You do not need to be an athlete, a coach or a race organiser.

## Event support

Helping deliver OCRA events, National Championships and qualification
competitions: athlete registration, course support, start and finish areas,
athlete information, medal ceremonies, equipment, logistics, volunteer
coordination and general operations.

This is the most direct way in, and it is where most people start.

## Technical officiating

If the sporting and technical side of competition appeals to you,
[Technical Officials](/education/technical-officials) are the people who keep the
rules applied fairly and consistently. It has a development pathway of its own and
it goes as far as international competition.

## Team Ireland support

Major championships need support beyond the competition itself — team
administration, athlete communications, logistics, team activities, media and
general championship support.

## Clubs and community

Local work counts as much as national: helping [clubs](/clubs/directory), community
activities, training groups, youth programmes, club events and local development.

## OCRA projects

As OCRA develops, so does the range of things that need doing —
communications, social media, photography and media, website and technology, event
planning, sponsorship, club development, education, administration and research.

You do not need previous sports administration experience. Skills from outside the
sport are often exactly what is missing.

## One event or an ongoing role

Some people want to help at a single race. Others want to take on an area
properly. Both are genuinely useful, and nobody will push you from the first
towards the second.

## If the role involves young people

Volunteer roles working with children or young people may require safeguarding
training, Garda Vetting or other requirements. These are explained before you
start, not after — see [safeguarding](/governance/safeguarding).

## Interested?

[Tell us](/about/contact) a little about yourself, what interests you and how much
time you have. If there is no immediate role, OCRA keeps your interest on record
for when there is.
