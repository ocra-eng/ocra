Where an international championship requires athletes to qualify through their
national federation, OCRA runs the Irish route. Requirements differ by
championship, discipline and category — there is no single rule covering all of
them.

## The current Irish route

Where qualification is required, OCRA-organised qualification events are the
recognised Irish pathway.

:::before-you-rely-on-this
Racing an independent event does **not** qualify you, even when that event
appears on the OCRA calendar. Approved Qualification Events are identified as
such *before* the competition. If a listing does not say it, it is not one.
:::

OCRA wants to widen the qualification calendar. Organisers can apply through
[Event Recognition & Sanctioning](/race-organisers/sanctioning).

## What the criteria will tell you

When a championship requires qualification, OCRA publishes the criteria for Irish
athletes: which events count, which disciplines, categories and Age Groups are
eligible, how qualification is decided, how many places exist, and the
registration deadline.

## When you do not need to qualify

Some international competitions allow Irish athletes to enter directly. That can
apply to a whole championship or only to certain categories. Where it does, you
enter under the championship organiser's own rules.

You may still need to satisfy requirements on nationality, age, category,
membership and the registration deadline.

## Qualifying is not always selection

Where more Irish athletes qualify than there are places, a further selection
process may be needed. Any additional criteria are published with the
championship information.

## Age Groups and categories

International category structures and age calculation methods change between
championships. Use the category information published for the championship you
are entering, not the one you entered last time.

## After you qualify

OCRA confirms your qualification and sets out what happens next: registration
instructions and deadline, any [Team Ireland](/compete/team-ireland)
requirements, the athlete information needed, championship fees where they apply,
and the competition information itself.

Completing registration and returning information by the deadline is the
athlete's responsibility.

## Not sure whether a result counts?

Ask before you rely on it. If you are unsure whether your event, category or
result is eligible, [contact us](/about/contact) — a much cheaper conversation
before the entry deadline than after it.
