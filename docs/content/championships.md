Championship racing is where Irish obstacle sport competes for national titles,
and where Irish athletes line up internationally.

## Irish National Championships

National Championships are run by OCRA directly, or with an approved race
organiser. Depending on the programme they cover short-format obstacle racing,
longer courses, Age Group, Elite and youth competition.

Current championships are identified in the [events calendar](/compete/events).

## International championships

Irish athletes may compete at European, World and other recognised international
championships. Entry rules differ between them: some require qualification or a
national federation nomination, others allow direct registration in particular
categories.

:::before-you-rely-on-this
Requirements change between championships and between seasons. Use the criteria
published for the championship you intend to enter — not last year's. See
[Qualification](/compete/qualification).
:::

## What each championship listing tells you

Every championship, national or international, is published with the same facts
so you can tell in one read whether it is for you: the dates and location; the
disciplines, categories and Age Groups; whether you are eligible and whether
qualification is required; how to register and by when; the rules that apply; and
the schedule, awards and how results will be published.

Where a championship involves selection, the [Team Ireland](/compete/team-ireland)
requirements are published with it.

## Archive

OCRA keeps a record of previous national, European and World-level competitions
involving Irish athletes — team selections, [results](/compete/results), medals,
and the rulebooks and qualification criteria that applied at the time.

Those historical criteria applied only to the season they were published for.
They are not current requirements.
