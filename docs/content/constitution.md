OCRA is governed by two documents. The Constitution — referred to in the
governing documents as the Statutes — establishes what the organisation is and
what it exists to do. The Bylaws are the operational rules that put it into
practice.

:::not-live
This page summarises both documents. The approved texts are not yet published
here. Until they are, this summary is a guide to what they cover, **not** the
instrument itself — if you need the binding wording,
[ask us](/about/contact) for the current approved version.
:::

## What the Constitution establishes

OCRA's name and legal form, its purpose and objectives, its activities,
membership, the governance structure, the General Assembly, how the organisation
is managed, committees, finance, transparency and integrity, how the Constitution
itself may be amended, and what happens on dissolution.

## The objectives it commits OCRA to

- Promote, develop and govern obstacle sport throughout Ireland
- Represent Ireland in international obstacle sport
- Establish national standards for competition, coaching and Technical
  Officiating
- Organise National Championships and qualification pathways
- Support athletes, clubs, coaches and Technical Officials
- Promote integrity, fairness, athlete welfare and inclusion
- Work with national and international sporting organisations

## What the Bylaws cover

Membership procedures and member rights, General Assembly procedure,
organisational management, committees, Technical Officials, coaching,
disciplinary processes, event standards, communication and records, and how the
Bylaws themselves are amended.

The Bylaws supplement the Constitution and are read consistently with it. Where
they appear to conflict, the Constitution governs.

## Changing them

Both may be amended only through the procedures they set out, and significant
changes require approval through OCRA's formal
[governance](/governance/structure) processes.

When a new version is approved it is published here with its effective date, and
the version it replaces moves to the archive marked as no longer current.
