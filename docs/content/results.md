OCRA keeps the results of its own competitions, the Irish National
Championships, qualification events, and Irish performances at international
championships. A result should still be findable long after the day it was set.

## Recent results

Each result identifies the athlete, their discipline, category and Age Group,
their overall and category position, and their time or score. Where penalties
applied, they are shown.

## Official and provisional

Results are published as `PROVISIONAL` immediately after a competition, which
leaves room for corrections, protests and reviews. Once that window closes they
are confirmed as `OFFICIAL`.

Check which one you are looking at before treating a result as final.

## National Championship results

OCRA keeps a permanent record of Irish National Champions, along with silver and
bronze medallists, and results by Age Group, by Elite category and by discipline.

## Team Ireland results

Performances by athletes representing Ireland at international championships form
part of Ireland's obstacle sport record, and are kept in both this archive and
the [Team Ireland](/compete/team-ireland) section.

## Archive

Historical results stay available by year and by event — OCRA competitions,
National Championships, qualification events, and European and World
Championships.

As the archive develops, an athlete will be able to see their own results across
multiple competitions in one place.

## Corrections

If you believe a result has been recorded incorrectly,
[tell us](/about/contact) with the event, athlete, discipline, category and the
result you want reviewed. Corrections are checked against the official
competition records.

## Rankings

:::not-live
OCRA is building a national ranking system. Nothing in this section is in effect
yet — **no results are currently being ranked**, and no ranking affects
qualification for anything. The first ranking season, the eligible competitions
and the points system will be published once the structure is settled.
:::

Irish obstacle racing happens across many events and many organisers, which
makes the season hard to see as a whole. A ranking is intended to connect those
races into one picture, and give athletes a way to measure a year rather than a
single race.

### Which events will count

Not every event on this site will feed the rankings. Only events approved as
OCRA Ranking Events will be eligible, and they will be labelled as such in the
[events calendar](/compete/events).

Likely to be eligible: OCRA-organised events, OCRA Sanctioned Events, Irish
National Championships and OCRA Qualification Events.

### How it will be structured

Rankings will be kept separately by discipline, category and Age Group, so that
athletes are compared within a structure that makes sense rather than forcing
100m and Standard Course into one table.

When the methodology is published it will set out which events are eligible, how
points are awarded, how many results count towards a total, whether National
Championships carry extra weight, and how ties break.

### Rankings and qualification

A ranking position may become part of selection in future, but only where the
[qualification criteria](/compete/qualification) for a specific championship say
so. At present none do.

### Historical rankings

Once the system launches, final standings from each season stay available
permanently — both as a record of how competitive OCR developed in Ireland, and
as recognition for the athletes who led their categories.
